BeyondAlarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

BeyondAlarm does not require an account and does not collect personal information directly. Alarm, Pomodoro, and settings data is stored locally on your device.

iCloud Backup (optional)

If you turn on backup, your data is saved to your own iCloud Drive. We do not have access to it.

Advertising

This app shows ads via Google AdMob, including a banner ad and optional rewarded video ads. Google and its partners may use device identifiers for ad delivery and, where applicable, personalization. In the EEA/UK, we ask for your consent via Google's User Messaging Platform before showing personalized ads.

See Google's own privacy policy for how they handle this data:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

BeyondAlarm is not directed at children under 13.
